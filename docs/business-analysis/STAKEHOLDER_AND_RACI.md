# Stakeholder Engagement and RACI

## Engagement Objective

Give each affected group a defined decision, contribution, and communication
path. Role names are used because this is a simulated portfolio programme.

## Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder group | Primary concern | Required involvement | Evidence produced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Service continuity, value, risk | Approve charter and escalated decisions | Charter and decision log |
| Business owners | Correct outputs and workable process | Define needs, prioritise, accept | Requirements and sign-off |
| Finance and operations users | Reporting deadlines, usability, continuity | Discovery, prototype review, UAT | Process maps and UAT results |
| Enterprise systems | Supportability and integration | Architecture, access, transition | Solution and support acceptance |
| Data platform team | Build quality and operability | Design, build, test, remediate | Technical evidence |
| Information security and privacy | Access, logging, sensitive data | Control review and approval | Security acceptance |
| Service desk | Support readiness | Knowledge transfer and hypercare | Support model and runbook |
| Vendor or consultant | Product constraints and specialist delivery | Design clarification and implementation support | Decisions and action log |

## RACI Matrix

| Activity | Sponsor | Business owner | Programme manager | Business analyst | Technical lead | Security | Service desk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approve charter and scope | A | C | R | C | C | I | I |
| Confirm artifact ownership and criticality | I | A | C | R | C | I | I |
| Facilitate discovery and process workshops | I | C | C | A/R | C | I | I |
| Baseline requirements and acceptance criteria | I | A | C | R | C | C | I |
| Approve wave plan and schedule | C | C | A/R | C | C | I | I |
| Design and build target solution | I | C | C | C | A/R | C | I |
| Approve security controls | I | C | I | C | R | A | I |
| Coordinate and execute UAT | I | A | C | R | C | I | I |
| Maintain RAID and decision logs | I | C | A | R | C | C | I |
| Prepare communications and training | I | A | C | R | C | I | C |
| Make GO or NO-GO recommendation | C | A | R | R | C | C | C |
| Execute cutover and rollback if required | I | C | A | C | R | C | R |
| Accept transition into support | I | C | C | C | R | I | A |

**R:** Responsible, **A:** Accountable, **C:** Consulted, **I:** Informed.

## Workshop Plan

| Workshop | Participants | Decisions and outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and service inventory | Business owners, users, technical lead | Owner, purpose, consumers, deadlines, pain points |
| Current-state workflow | Users, service desk, business analyst | Trigger-to-output map, exceptions, manual controls |
| Requirements and options | Business owner, technical lead, security | Priorities, constraints, acceptance criteria, option decision |
| Future-state and change impact | Users, service desk, technical lead | New workflow, role impacts, training and support needs |
| UAT readiness | Business owner, testers, technical lead | Test scope, test data, entry and exit criteria |
| GO or NO-GO | Business owner, programme manager, technical and support leads | Recorded decision, conditions, owner and due date |

## Facilitation Controls

- Circulate purpose, decisions required, and pre-reading before each workshop.
- Record decisions separately from discussion notes.
- Assign every action an owner and date before closing the meeting.
- Send outputs for correction within two working days.
- Escalate unresolved scope, security, or service-continuity questions through
  the governance path in the charter.
